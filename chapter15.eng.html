Module 15: Electrical Safety

Electricity is essential in industry, but it can cause electric shock, burns, fire or serious injury within seconds.

Learning Objectives

⚡ Recognise key electrical hazards.
🔥 Understand the consequences of electric shock, burns and arc flash.
🔌 Inspect cables, plugs, sockets and portable electrical equipment before use.
🚨 Know what to do and what not to do during an electrical incident.

Why is Electrical Safety Important?

Electricity is not always visible, but its consequences can be immediate and severe. Minor cable damage, incorrect work on an electrical panel or using equipment in wet conditions can quickly lead to a serious incident.

Critical message: Only authorised and appropriately trained persons should work on electrical panels or electrical installations.

Key Electrical Hazards

⚡

Electric Shock

Electric current can pass through the body and cause serious injury or death.

🔥

Burns

Electrical arcs, heat or contact with electrical equipment can cause burns.

💥

Arc Flash

A sudden release of electrical energy involving intense heat, light, pressure and flying debris.

🔌

Damaged Cables

Cut, exposed or crushed cables increase the risk of electric shock and short circuits.

💧

Water and Moisture

Water and moisture significantly increase the risk of electric shock.

🚫

Unauthorised Intervention

Opening electrical panels, temporary repairs or bypassing protective systems are unsafe practices.

Pre-Use Inspection

1. Cable

Check for cuts, exposed conductors, kinks or signs of overheating.

2. Plug and Socket

Check for broken parts, loose connections or signs of burning.

3. Equipment

Do not use equipment that smells burnt, makes unusual noises or operates erratically.

4. Environment

Do not use electrical equipment near water or moisture unless suitable protection is provided.

If in doubt, do not use the equipment. Mark it as out of service and report it to the responsible person.

What to Do / What Not to Do

✅

Do

Report damaged equipment, keep others away from the hazard and use only electrical equipment that is in good condition.

✅

Do

Follow the required energy-isolation procedures where applicable.

❌

Do Not

Do not open electrical panels unless you are authorised to do so.

❌

Do Not

Do not touch a person who may still be in contact with electrical current.

Key Takeaways

Electricity can be fatal even when the hazard is not visible.

Never use damaged cables, plugs, sockets or electrical tools.

Only authorised personnel should work on electrical panels and installations.

Water and electricity are a dangerous combination.

During an electric-shock incident, do not touch the person until the electrical source has been isolated and the situation is safe.
