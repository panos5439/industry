Module 22: Safety Signage

Safety signage uses colours, shapes and symbols to help us recognise hazards, prohibitions, mandatory actions and safe routes.

Learning Objectives

⚠️ Recognise the main categories of safety signs.
🎨 Correctly associate colour and shape with the sign message.
🚪 Recognise escape and safety signs.
📢 Report signs that are missing, damaged or not visible.

22.1 What is Safety Signage?

Safety signage is a system of symbols, colours and shapes that helps us quickly understand what hazard exists and what action is required.

Key message: Safety signage provides an immediate visual message, but it does not replace training, safe procedures or protective measures.

22.2 General Rules

👀

Know the Signs

Know the safety signs that are present in your workplace.

✅

Follow the Instructions

Follow the instruction, prohibition or mandatory action indicated by the sign.

📢

Report Problems

If a sign is missing, damaged or not visible, report it to the responsible person.

🚫

Do Not Remove Signage

Do not cover, move or remove safety signs and labels.

22.3 Categories of Safety Signs

Shape and colour help us immediately recognise the type of message.

Warning Signs

Yellow triangle – warns of a hazard (e.g. risk of electric shock).

Prohibition Signs

Red circle with a diagonal line – something is prohibited (e.g. smoking).

Mandatory Signs

Blue circle – an action must be carried out (e.g. wearing gloves).

Escape / Safety Signs

Green rectangle – indicates an emergency exit or first-aid facilities.

Fire-Fighting Equipment Signs

Red square – indicates fire extinguishers, fire hose points, etc.

Important: Know the signs that are present in your workplace and always follow the instruction or restriction they communicate.

22.4 Special Labels and Markings

🧪

Chemical Substances

Each container should retain its required label.

Do not remove or alter hazard labels.

🚪

Exits and Escape Routes

Exit signage should remain visible and escape routes should remain free from obstructions.

⚡

Electrical Panels

Electrical installations may carry specific warning signage.

🦺

Mandatory PPE

Signage may indicate specific PPE that must be used before entry or work begins.

22.5 Practical Tips

🚪

Know the Exit

Know where the nearest emergency exit is located.

💧

Do Not Ignore Warnings

If temporary signage is in place for a slippery floor or another hazardous condition, follow it.

🧯

Keep Signage Visible

Fire-fighting equipment signage should remain clearly visible.

📢

Report Damage

If a sign is missing or damaged, report it immediately.
