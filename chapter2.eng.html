Module 2: Hazard Identification

Every accident starts with a hazard. The sooner we identify it, the easier it is to control it.

Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, the worker will be able to:

👀 Identify hazards in the workplace.
⚠️ Distinguish between a Hazard and a Risk.
🧠 Apply the Look – Think – Act method.
🗣️ Report unsafe conditions before an accident occurs.

Hazard and Risk

⚡

Hazard

Anything with the potential to cause injury, illness, damage or another unwanted consequence.

📊

Risk

The combination of the likelihood that a hazardous event will occur and the severity of its potential consequences.

Example: Liquid on the floor is the hazard. The possibility that someone may slip and be injured represents the risk.

Look – Think – Act Method

👀 LOOK Look at the workplace, equipment, tools and the people around you.
🧠 THINK Think about what could go wrong and who could be injured.
✅ ACT Take action, report the hazard or stop the work when it cannot be carried out safely.

Key Takeaways

Every accident starts with a hazard.

Early hazard identification helps prevent injuries.

A Hazard is a source of potential harm. Risk reflects the likelihood and severity of that harm.

Apply Look – Think – Act before every task.

If you see something unsafe, speak up and report it.
