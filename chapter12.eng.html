Module 12: Emergency Response

In an emergency, the correct response during the first few minutes can protect lives, limit damage and support safe evacuation.

Learning Objectives

🚨Recognise basic types of emergency situations.
📢Know how to raise the alarm and notify others.
🚪Follow a safe evacuation procedure.
📍Move correctly to the assembly point.

What is an emergency?

An emergency is any situation that may immediately threaten people, facilities, equipment or the environment and requires an immediate, organised response.

🔥

Fire or smoke

Fire, burning smell, smoke or activation of the alarm.

🧪

Chemical spill

Leak, vapour, splash or unknown substance.

🚑

Injury

Serious injury, unconsciousness or need for first aid.

⚡

Technical incident

Electrical fault, explosion, gas leak or equipment failure.

Basic response principle

Life first, equipment second. Do not try to save materials, tools or production when people may be at risk.

1. Stop

Do not act impulsively. Quickly assess whether there is immediate danger.

2. Notify

Activate the alarm or notify according to the site procedure.

3. Evacuate

Use the safe escape route.

4. Assemble

Go to the designated assembly point and report your presence.

Evacuation

When an evacuation order is given or the alarm sounds, move immediately and calmly.

🚶

Walk, do not run

Running may cause falls or panic.

🚫

Do not go back

Do not return for personal belongings, tools or equipment.

🟢

Follow signage

Use designated escape routes and emergency exits.

📍

Assembly point

Remain at the assembly point until instructions are given.

Do not use elevators during evacuation. Use stairs or approved escape routes.

What should I do in different incidents?

🔥

Fire / smoke

Raise the alarm, move away, close doors if safe and do not intervene unless trained.

🧪

Chemical spill

Avoid exposure, do not touch unknown substances, mark the area and notify.

🚑

Injury

Call first aid. Do not move the injured person unless there is immediate danger.

⚡

Electrical incident

Do not touch a person or equipment that may be energised. Notify immediately.

Key Takeaways

In an emergency, human life comes first.

Raise the alarm immediately according to the procedure.

Follow escape routes and go to the assembly point.

Do not return to a dangerous area for personal belongings.

Wait for instructions before returning to the workplace.
