Module 9: Slips, Trips and Falls

Slips, trips and falls may appear to be simple incidents, but they can cause serious injuries, fractures and time away from work.

Learning Objectives

👣 Distinguish between slips, trips and falls.
⚠️ Recognise common causes of falls.
🧹 Apply good housekeeping in the workplace.
🗣️ Report spills, obstructions and unsafe conditions immediately.

Slip, Trip, Fall

🧊

Slip

A slip occurs when the foot loses traction with the floor surface.

Example: Oil, water, dust or a slippery surface.

🔌

Trip

A trip occurs when the foot strikes an obstruction and the body loses balance.

Example: A cable, pallet, tool or uneven floor surface.

⬇️

Fall

A fall occurs when the body drops to the same level or from a height.

Example: Falling from a ladder, platform or onto the production floor.

Key message: Many falls begin with small everyday hazards that were not corrected in time.

Common Causes

💧

Spills

Water, oil, grease, chemicals or cleaning fluids on the floor.

📦

Obstructions

Boxes, pallets, tools or materials left in walkways.

🔌

Cables

Cables crossing walkways or work areas.

💡

Poor Lighting

Shadows, low lighting or failed lamps.

🪜

Unsafe Ladder Use

A ladder positioned incorrectly, unstable or not checked before use.

👟

Unsuitable Footwear

Footwear with poor grip or footwear in poor condition.

Prevention in Practice

🧹

Clear Walkways

Keep walkways, exits and access points free from obstructions.

🟡

Immediate Warning

Mark spills or wet areas immediately until they are controlled.

🧽

Cleaning

Clean the spill or arrange for it to be cleaned safely.

📢

Reporting

Report floor damage, poor lighting, obstructions and unsafe conditions.

Remember: Good housekeeping is not about appearance. It is a fundamental accident-prevention measure.

Safe Ladder Use

🔍

Inspect Before Use

Check for damage, broken rungs, slippery feet or instability.

📐

Correct Positioning

The ladder must be stable and positioned appropriately for the type of ladder and task.

🤲

Three Points of Contact

Maintain three points of contact when climbing or descending where this is applicable.

🚫

Do Not Overreach

Do not lean excessively to reach a distant point. Climb down and reposition the ladder.

Key Takeaways

💧
Small spills can cause serious injuries.
🚶
Clear, unobstructed walkways support safer movement.
📢
Spills and other hazards should be reported immediately.
🔌
Cables, tools and materials should not create obstructions in walkways.
🪜
Use ladders correctly, maintain safe contact and do not overreach.
