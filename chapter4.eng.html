Module 4: Personal Protective Equipment

PPE is important, but it does not automatically make a task safe. It protects workers when hazards cannot be fully eliminated.

Learning Objectives

🦺 Understand the role of PPE in worker protection.
🛡️ Recognise the main categories of PPE.
🔍 Inspect PPE before use.
✅ Select suitable PPE according to the task.

What is PPE?

Personal Protective Equipment is equipment worn or used by a worker to reduce exposure to hazards that cannot be fully eliminated.

Important: PPE does not remove the hazard. It reduces the likelihood or severity of injury when used correctly.

Why is PPE the last level of protection?

According to the hierarchy of controls, we should first try to eliminate or reduce the hazard at the source. PPE is used when exposure still remains.

Example: If there is a risk of flying particles, safety glasses are necessary. However, engineering controls such as guards, covers or isolation of the source should also be considered first.

Main Categories of PPE

⛑️

Head Protection

Safety helmet for falling objects or impact hazards.

🥽

Eye Protection

Safety glasses or face shield for particles, chemicals or splashes.

🎧

Hearing Protection

Earplugs or earmuffs in high-noise areas.

😷

Respiratory Protection

Masks or respirators for dust, vapours or gases.

🧤

Hand Protection

Gloves selected according to the hazard: cuts, heat, chemicals or abrasion.

🥾

Foot Protection

Safety footwear for impact, slipping or puncture hazards.

🦺

Body Protection

High-visibility vests, coveralls, aprons or special protective clothing.

🧗

Fall Protection

Harnesses, lanyards and fall-arrest systems for work at height.

Inspection Before Use

🔍

Is it in good condition?

Check for damage, cracks, tears, broken parts or expiry dates.

📏

Does it fit correctly?

PPE must fit properly and must not interfere with safe work.

🏷️

Is it suitable for the hazard?

Not all gloves, filters or glasses are suitable for every task.

🧼

Is it clean and maintained?

Dirty or poorly maintained PPE may lose its effectiveness.

Key Takeaways

PPE protects only when it is correctly selected, correctly worn and in good condition.

PPE does not remove the hazard. It reduces exposure.

Inspect PPE before every use.

Do not start work with damaged or unsuitable PPE.
