Module 6: Machine Safety

Machines support production, but moving parts, cutting points, trapping points and crushing zones can cause serious injuries.

Learning Objectives

⚙️ Recognise basic machine hazards.
🛡️ Understand the purpose of machine guards.
🛑 Understand the importance of the emergency stop.
✅ Never bypass guards or safety procedures.

Why is Machine Safety Important?

Industrial machinery may include rotating parts, blades, shafts, rollers, cutting points, crushing points and trapping zones, as well as electrical, mechanical, hydraulic or pneumatic energy.

Why it matters: Contact with a moving or cutting part can cause serious injury in a fraction of a second. Never reach into, clean, adjust or clear a machine without following the required safe procedure.

Main Machine Hazards

🔄

Rotating Parts

Shafts, chucks, rollers, belts and other rotating components can catch clothing, hair or other loose items.

✂️

Cutting Points

Saws, blades, cutting tools and presses can cause severe injuries.

🧲

Trapping Points

Two moving parts, or one moving part and one fixed part, can trap hands, fingers or other parts of the body.

🔥

Hot Surfaces

Machines and materials may reach temperatures capable of causing burns.

⚡

Stored Energy

Electrical, mechanical, hydraulic or pneumatic energy may remain in the machine even after it has stopped.

🚫

Bypassing Guards

Removing, disabling or bypassing guards immediately increases the risk.

Machine Guards

Machine guards prevent or limit worker access to dangerous moving or cutting parts.

Rule: If a guard is missing, damaged, displaced or bypassed, the machine should not be used until a safe condition has been restored.

Emergency Stop

The emergency stop is used to stop a machine quickly during an emergency situation.

Remember: The emergency stop should be known and accessible, but it does not replace the approved safe shutdown and energy-isolation procedure required before intervention.

Key Takeaways

⚙️
Moving and cutting parts can cause serious injury in a very short time.
🛡️
Never remove, move or bypass machine guards.
🛑
The emergency stop is for emergencies and does not replace energy isolation.
🔒
Before cleaning, adjustment or clearing a jam, follow the approved safe procedure.
