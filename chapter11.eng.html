Module 11: Fire Safety

Fire safety in industry is based on prevention, early recognition of hazards and the correct response in case of fire.

Learning Objectives

🔥Understand how a fire starts and spreads.
🧯Recognise basic types of fire extinguishers.
🚨Know when to evacuate and raise the alarm.
🚪Follow a safe evacuation procedure.

The Fire Triangle

For a fire to exist, three elements are required. If one of them is removed, the fire cannot continue.

🔥Heat

Sparks, flame, hot surfaces, friction or electrical faults.

🪵Fuel

Paper, wood, plastic, oil, gases, chemicals or dust.

🌬️Oxygen

Air supports combustion and helps the fire continue.

Basic idea: Fire prevention means controlling ignition sources, combustible materials and unsafe conditions.

Common Causes of Fire in Industry

⚡

Electrical problems

Overloads, damaged cables, poor connections or unauthorised modifications.

🔥

Hot work

Welding, cutting, grinding and sparks near flammable materials.

🧪

Flammable chemicals

Incorrect storage, open containers or use near ignition sources.

🧹

Poor housekeeping

Accumulation of waste, dust, paper, oils or combustible materials.

Fire Prevention

🚫

Reduce fuel load

Remove unnecessary combustible materials from the work area.

🔌

Control electrical equipment

Do not use damaged cables, overloaded power strips or unauthorised equipment.

🧯

Keep fire equipment accessible

Do not block extinguishers, fire hose reels or emergency exits.

📋

Hot work permit

For welding, cutting or grinding, follow the approved procedure.

Types of Fire Extinguishers

💧

Water

For solid combustibles such as wood, paper and fabrics. Do not use on electrical equipment.

🧯

Dry Powder

Suitable for many fire classes. It may reduce visibility in enclosed areas.

☁️

CO₂

Often used for electrical equipment. It leaves no residue.

🫧

Foam

For flammable liquids and solid materials. Not suitable for all electrical incidents.

Important: Use a fire extinguisher only if you are trained, the fire is small, you have a safe exit behind you and there is no smoke or immediate danger.

The PASS Method

If you are trained and it is safe to use a fire extinguisher, remember the PASS method.

P — Pull

Pull the safety pin.

A — Aim

Aim at the base of the fire.

S — Squeeze

Squeeze the handle.

S — Sweep

Sweep from side to side at the base of the fire.

Key Takeaways

Prevention is the best form of fire safety.

Do not block fire extinguishers, emergency exits or fire hose reels.

Use a fire extinguisher only if it is safe and you are trained.

If there is smoke, unknown material or rapid fire spread, evacuate and raise the alarm.

No attempt to fight a fire is worth more than human life.
