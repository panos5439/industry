Module 7: Lockout / Tagout – LOTO

Energy isolation protects employees from unexpected startup, movement, or energy release during maintenance, cleaning, or other work on equipment.

Learning Objectives

🔒 Understand what LOTO is and why it is used.
⚡ Recognize hazardous forms of energy.
🏷️ Understand the purpose of locks and tags.
✅ Follow the basic steps of safe energy isolation.

What Is LOTO?

Lockout / Tagout is a safety procedure used to isolate equipment from its energy sources before maintenance, cleaning, adjustment, repair, or unblocking work begins.

Simple explanation: Lockout means physically locking an energy-isolating device so that it cannot be operated. Tagout means attaching a warning tag that identifies who applied the isolation and why.

Why Is LOTO Important?

Equipment that appears to be stopped may still contain active or stored energy. Unexpected startup, movement, or energy release can cause serious or fatal injury.

Critical message: A STOP button is not LOTO. An emergency stop is not LOTO. Isolation requires an approved procedure, locking, tagging, and verification of a zero-energy state.

Types of Hazardous Energy

⚡

Electrical Energy

Electrical panels, wiring, motors, batteries, and power supplies.

⚙️

Mechanical Energy

Moving parts, springs, rotating components, elevated loads, and gravity.

💧

Hydraulic Energy

Pressurized fluids, hydraulic cylinders, accumulators, and hydraulic lines.

🌬️

Pneumatic Energy

Compressed air, valves, cylinders, receivers, and pneumatic lines.

🔥

Thermal Energy

Hot surfaces, steam, heated liquids, gases, and materials.

🧪

Chemical Energy

Pressurized chemicals, hazardous reactions, leaks, gases, vapors, and corrosive substances.

When Is LOTO Required?

LOTO may be required whenever an employee must work on equipment where unexpected startup, movement, or energy release could cause injury.

🔧

Maintenance

Repairs, component replacement, inspections, and technical interventions.

🧹

Cleaning

Cleaning near moving parts, hazardous areas, or internal machine components.

🚧

Unblocking

Removing jammed or blocked materials from machinery or production lines.

⚙️

Adjustment

Adjusting or working on equipment where movement or energy release may occur.

Basic LOTO Steps

1. Prepare

Understand the equipment, its energy sources, potential hazards, and the approved procedure.

2. Notify

Inform affected employees that the equipment will be shut down and isolated.

3. Shut Down

Stop the equipment using its normal shutdown procedure.

4. Isolate Energy

Disconnect or isolate every hazardous energy source.

5. Apply Locks and Tags

Apply personal locks and identification tags to each energy-isolating device.

6. Release Stored Energy

Relieve pressure, discharge capacitors, lower elevated loads, block movement, and drain lines where required.

7. Verify Zero Energy

Confirm that the equipment cannot start, move, or release hazardous energy.

8. Perform the Work

Begin the work only after the equipment has been verified as safe.

Never remove another employee’s personal LOTO lock. A personal lock should only be removed by the employee who applied it, except through a formally approved removal procedure.
