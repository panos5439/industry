Manual Call Points

Manual call points allow the fire alarm to be activated manually when someone notices fire, smoke or an immediate danger.

Learning Objectives

🚨 Understand what a manual call point is.
📍 Recognise where manual call points are usually located.
🔥 Know when they should be used.
👆 Understand how they are activated depending on their type.
🚪 Know what actions should follow after activation.

1. What Is a Manual Call Point?

A manual call point is a device used to manually activate the fire alarm.

👆

Manual Activation

It allows a person to activate the alarm when they notice fire, smoke or another immediate danger.

📡

Signal to the Panel

After activation, it sends a signal to the fire detection or alarm control panel.

🔔

Alarm Activation

Depending on the system, sirens or beacons may be activated.

Basic logic: Call Point → Inform → Evacuate.

3. Where Is It Usually Located?

🚪

Near Emergency Exits

It is often located near emergency exits and escape routes.

🪜

Stairwells

It may be located near stairwells and evacuation points.

🏢

Corridors & Common Areas

It may be installed in corridors and common areas.

👁️

Visible & Accessible

It should not be blocked by objects or furniture.

4. When Do We Use the Call Point?

🔥

When We See Fire

We use it when we see fire and people in the area need to be warned immediately.

💨

When We See Smoke

It can be activated when smoke is detected, even if the automatic alarm has not yet sounded.

⚠️

Immediate Danger

It is used when there is an immediate danger and the alarm has not activated.

🚶

During Evacuation

If, during evacuation, we pass a call point and the alarm has not sounded, it may be activated.

5. How Do We Use It?

1
ACTIVATE Press the call point or activate its mechanism, depending on the type.
2
INFORM Immediately warn people nearby.
3
CALL 112 Call 112 or assign another person to make the call.
4
EVACUATE Follow the green exit signs and proceed to the assembly point.
Rule: Call Point → Inform → Evacuate.

6. What Do We Do After Activation?

📢

Inform Others

Immediately warn people nearby and follow the alarm procedure.

📞

Emergency Call

The module material includes calling 112 or assigning the call to another person.

🟢

Follow the Signs

Proceed according to the green escape route signs.

📍

Assembly Point

Complete the evacuation at the designated assembly point.

7. False Activation

Use only in a real emergency. False activation may cause panic and disrupt the safety response.
