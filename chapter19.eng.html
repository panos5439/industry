Module 19: Overhead Crane & Pallet Storage Safety

Safe lifting operations and correct pallet storage require suitable equipment, correct load attachment, effective control of the danger zone and continuous monitoring of load stability.

Why is Lifting Safety Important?

A suspended load can contain a large amount of potential energy. Failure of lifting equipment, incorrect attachment or the presence of a worker in the danger zone can result in serious or fatal injury.

Why it matters: No person should ever stand beneath a suspended load.
Important: The capacity of the overhead crane is not the only consideration. The weight of the load, lifting accessories, attachment points, centre of gravity and the actual travel path of the load must also be considered.

Main Hazards

⬇️ Falling Load

Attachment Failure

Unsuitable or damaged slings, hooks or lifting points may result in the load falling.

⚖️ Instability

Unbalanced Load

An incorrect centre of gravity can cause the load to tilt, shift or become uncontrolled.

👷 People

Presence in the Danger Zone

Standing below or too close to a moving load creates a serious risk of crushing or impact.

🏗️ Equipment

Exceeding Rated Capacity

Lifting a load above the permitted capacity can cause serious equipment failure.

📦 Storage

Unstable Pallets

Pallets that overhang, lean or are damaged may fall from the storage system.

🗄️ Racking

Racking Overload

Exceeding permitted loads or incorrect pallet placement may cause failure of the storage system.

Checks Before Lifting

Overhead Crane

  • The equipment is suitable for the task.
  • The rated capacity is known.
  • The hook and hoisting mechanism are visually checked.
  • The controls operate correctly.
  • There is no visible damage or unusual noise.
  • The load travel path is clear.

Lifting Accessories

  • Slings are suitable for the load.
  • There are no cuts or visible signs of damage.
  • The lifting capacity of accessories is suitable.
  • Connection points are secure.
  • The load can be lifted in balance.
  • Sharp edges will not damage the slings.

Safe Lifting Process

1. Plan

Determine the load weight, dimensions, centre of gravity and planned travel path.

2. Select Equipment

Select a suitable overhead crane, hook and lifting accessories.

3. Attach the Load

Position slings at suitable points and confirm that the load is balanced.

4. Test Lift

Raise the load slightly and confirm that it remains stable.

5. Move the Load

Move the load slowly and smoothly, avoiding sudden movements.

6. Set Down

Place the load on a stable and suitable surface.

Exclusion Zone

No one beneath the load. The operator must maintain safe separation and ensure that no person enters the path or drop zone of the suspended load.
🚧

Area Control

Use suitable barriers or other access-control measures where required.

👀

Visibility

The operator must have adequate visibility or receive suitable guidance.

🗣️

Communication

Use clear and agreed signals when a signaller or banksman is involved.

🛑

Stop Work

Stop immediately if any person enters the danger zone.

Safe Pallet Storage

📐

Correct Positioning

The pallet should be correctly supported at the intended points and should not overhang dangerously.

📦

Stable Load

Products should be stable and suitably secured on the pallet.

⚖️

Weight

Permitted pallet and racking loads must not be exceeded.

🔍

Inspection

Damaged pallets and visibly deformed racking should be reported and assessed.

When Should Work Stop?

⛓️

Damaged Sling

When visible wear or damage is found on lifting accessories.

⚖️

Load Instability

When the load tilts, shifts or behaves unpredictably.

👷

Person Enters the Area

When any person enters the danger zone.

❓

Unknown Load Weight

When the weight of the load or the suitability of the lifting equipment has not been confirmed.

📦

Unstable Pallet

When there is a risk that a pallet or its contents may fall.

🔧

Equipment Fault

When abnormal operation, unusual noise or a technical defect is identified.

Key Takeaways

🏗️
Every lifting operation should be planned before it begins.
⛓️
Use only suitable and inspected lifting accessories.
🚧
No person should ever stand beneath a suspended load.
📦
Pallets must be stable, correctly positioned and stored within the limits of the racking system.
🛑
If there is uncertainty, instability or a change in conditions, stop and reassess before continuing.
