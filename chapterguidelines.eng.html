Quick User Guide

How do I use the platform?

Everything you need to know about tags, navigation in the 3D environment, language selection and the final exam.

★
Most important: open and review all tags. The final exam questions are based on their content.
✓

At a Glance

The correct order for using the platform.

1. Open the tags Review all training content.
2. Complete each section Select the completion checkbox.
3. Check the language Before starting the exam.
4. Take the exam From the relevant menu option.
1

Side Menu & Tags

Tags are the main learning points of the training.

What do they contain?

Information, instructions and, where applicable, short activities or questions.

What should you do?

  • Open all tags.
  • Follow them in order.
  • Do not skip any content.
!
Do not forget to confirm completion.
In each section, select the relevant checkbox at the bottom of the screen to confirm that you have completed it.
i
If you lose track of your progress, return to the side menu and continue from the next tag.
2

Navigation in the 3D Environment

The basic controls you will need.

Left click + drag Rotate the view
Mouse wheel Zoom in / out
Click a point / arrow keys Move around the environment
i
If a point does not respond, zoom out slightly and try again.
3

Language Selection

There are two different language settings.

Language inside a tag

The language option at the top-right of a tag changes only the text of that specific tag.

Side menu language

The language selected through the language icon in the side menu also determines the language of the final exam.

!
Before starting the exam: check that the side menu is set to your preferred language.
4

Final Exam

Start only after you have completed the training content.

1

Open the side menu

Find the “START THE EXAM” option.

2

Select the relevant tag

Click the tag that launches the final exam.

3

Answer the questions

The questions are based on the content of the training tags.

Pass Requirement

Successful completion requires a score of 70%, unless your organisation has set a different passing score.

Target: ≥ 70%
5

If Something Is Not Working Properly

Try these simple steps first.

1

Refresh the page

Refresh the page and allow the content to load again.

2

Wait a few seconds

The 3D environment may need a little time to load completely.

3

Restart or change your browser

If the problem continues, close and reopen your browser or try a different browser.
