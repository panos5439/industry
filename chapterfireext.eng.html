Portable Fire Extinguisher Maintenance & Use

A fire extinguisher is useful only when it is available, in good condition and ready to operate when it is actually needed. Proper inspection and maintenance are an essential part of fire safety preparedness.

Learning Objectives

🧯 Understand why proper maintenance is critical to the reliability of a portable fire extinguisher.
📅 Recognise the main inspection and maintenance intervals described in this module.
🔍 Know the main points of a quarterly visual inspection.
⚙️ Understand when extended inspection and agent replacement are required.
🏷️ Understand the importance of fire rating when selecting an extinguisher.

1. Why Is Maintenance Important?

A fire extinguisher is emergency equipment. Simply having one in the workplace is not enough. It must remain in a condition that allows it to operate reliably when required.

Key message: A fire extinguisher is not decoration. It is equipment that must remain available and operational.

2. The Maintenance Logic

According to the module material, monitoring a portable fire extinguisher involves different levels of inspection throughout its service life.

3 months
VISUAL INSPECTION The owner carries out a quarterly visual inspection.
1 year
ANNUAL MAINTENANCE Carried out by a recognised and certified maintenance company.
5 years
EXTINGUISHING AGENT The extinguishing agent is replaced according to the module schedule.
10+ years
EXTENDED INSPECTION An extended laboratory inspection may be required according to the module criteria.

3. Annual Maintenance

📅

Once a Year

According to the material, the extinguisher must be serviced once a year.

🛠️

By an Appropriate Company

Maintenance is carried out by a recognised and certified maintenance company.

✅

Objective

To keep the extinguisher in a condition that allows reliable and effective operation.

4. Quarterly Visual Inspection

In addition to annual maintenance, the material specifies a quarterly visual inspection by the owner.

🟢

Pressure Gauge

Check whether the pressure gauge needle is within the green zone.

🔎

Body Condition

Check for visible damage, rust or deformation.

📌

Safety Pin

Check that the safety pin is in position and intact.

Example of a fire extinguisher pressure gauge. According to the module material, the needle should be within the green zone.
If a deviation is identified: immediately contact the maintenance company for repair or replacement.

5. Extinguishing Agent

5️⃣

Every 5 Years

According to the original material, the extinguishing agent is replaced every 5 years.

🧯

After Use

The material states that replacement is also required after any use of the extinguisher.

Important: An extinguisher that has been used should not be treated as though it is automatically ready for further use.

6. Extended Laboratory Inspection

According to the material, when the extinguisher is more than 10 years old or when its overall condition requires it, an extended inspection is carried out.

🔩

Components

Replacement of worn components.

🧯

Extinguishing Agent

Replacement of the extinguishing agent.

💧

Hydraulic Test

A hydraulic test of the cylinder is carried out.

⚙️

Pressure System

A complete inspection of the pressure system is carried out.

7. Service Life of a Powder Fire Extinguisher

The provided material states a maximum service life of 20 years for powder fire extinguishers.

20 years: After this period, the material specifies withdrawal and replacement with a new extinguisher.

8. Fire Rating

The performance of a fire extinguisher is described by its fire rating and is shown on its label.

🏷️

Read the Label

The fire rating is important information displayed on the extinguisher label.

⚖️

Same Quantity ≠ Same Performance

Two extinguishers containing the same amount of agent do not necessarily have the same effectiveness.

🎯

Appropriate Selection

Fire rating should be considered when selecting the appropriate extinguisher.
