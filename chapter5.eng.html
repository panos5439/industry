Module 5: Safe Behaviour and Safety Culture

Safety does not depend only on equipment and procedures. It also depends on the daily choices made by every worker.

Learning Objectives

✅Recognise safe and unsafe behaviours.
⚠️Understand how human factors affect safety.
🗣️Speak up when unsafe work is observed.
🛑Apply the right to stop unsafe work.

What is safe behaviour?

Safe behaviour is any action that protects you, your colleagues, the equipment and the workplace.

📋

Follow procedures

Do not skip steps, even when the task seems simple.

🦺

Use PPE correctly

Wear it properly and inspect it before use.

🔧

Use the correct tools

Do not improvise with unsuitable or damaged tools.

🧹

Keep the area tidy

Clear walkways and organised work areas reduce accidents.

Unsafe Behaviour

Unsafe behaviour is any action or omission that increases the likelihood of an accident.

Examples: running in production areas, using a mobile phone while working, bypassing guards, horseplay, rushing, ignoring procedures or working without a permit where one is required.

Human Factors

Many accidents do not happen because workers “do not know”. They happen because something affects judgement, attention or behaviour.

😴

Fatigue

Reduces attention and increases mistakes.

⏱️

Time pressure

Can lead to rushing and shortcuts.

📱

Distraction

One second of inattention may be enough for an accident.

😐

Complacency

“I always do it this way” can hide a serious risk.

Remember: Experience helps, but it does not replace attention and the correct procedure.

Safe or Unsafe?

Read the behaviour and choose whether it is safe or unsafe.

Stop Work Authority

Every worker should be able to stop a task when they believe there is an immediate or serious danger.

Correct attitude: “We stop for a moment, check the risk and continue only when the task is safe.”

Key Takeaways

Safety is a daily behaviour.

Rushing and shortcuts increase the likelihood of an accident.

Speak up when you see something unsafe.

No task is so urgent that it cannot be done safely.
