Fire Detection: Smoke & Heat Detectors

Fire detection provides early warning when signs of fire appear. The aim is to notify people as early as possible so evacuation can begin quickly and safely.

Learning Objectives

🔥 Understand what fire detection is and what its purpose is.
💨 Recognise how a smoke detector works.
🌡️ Understand how a heat detector operates.
🔴 Understand the role of manual alarm call points.
🚪 Know what to do when the alarm activates.

1. What Is Fire Detection?

Fire detection is a system that identifies early signs of fire, such as smoke or heat.

The system can activate the alarm, so that people in the area are warned as early as possible.

Main purpose: early warning, so evacuation can begin quickly and safely.

2. How Does the System Work?

1
DETECTION The detector identifies smoke or heat.
2
SIGNAL It sends a signal to the fire alarm control panel.
3
ALARM Sirens or beacons are activated, where installed.
4
EVACUATION People move away toward emergency exits.

3. Smoke Detector

💨

What Does It Detect?

It detects smoke particles in the air.

⏱️

Early Activation

It can activate early when smoke appears, depending on the design and installation area.

🔔

Connection to the Panel

It sends a signal to the fire alarm control panel, which can activate sirens or beacons.

Illustrative photo of a smoke detector mounted on a ceiling.
Detectors are usually installed on the ceiling and connected to the fire alarm control panel.

4. Heat Detector

🌡️

High Temperature

It may activate when the temperature reaches a high level.

📈

Rapid Temperature Rise

It may also respond to a rapid increase in temperature.

🏭

Applications

It is often used in areas where steam or dust may be present, depending on the design.

🔗

Complementary Operation

It can operate alongside smoke detection for improved coverage.

5. Manual Alarm Call Points

Fire detection provides an automatic form of warning.

Manual alarm call points are used to provide a manual warning when someone sees fire or smoke.

If you see fire: activate the alarm, if available, and begin evacuation according to the site procedure.

6. What Do I Do When the Alarm Activates?

1
STOP Stay calm and immediately stop what you are doing.
2
FOLLOW THE EXIT Move toward the nearest emergency exit.
3
USE THE STAIRS Do not use elevators. Use the stairs.
4
ASSEMBLY POINT Go to the assembly point and wait for instructions.
Rule: Hear the alarm → move away → proceed toward the emergency exit.

7. Protecting Fire Detectors

🚫

Do Not Cover Them

Do not cover fire detectors, because this may affect their ability to detect a fire.

🎨

Do Not Paint Them

Do not paint detectors or their openings.

↔️

Do Not Move Them

Do not move detectors from their installed position.

📢

Report Damage

If damage or a fault is identified, it should be reported immediately.
